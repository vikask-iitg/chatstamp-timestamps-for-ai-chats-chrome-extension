About

Timestamps for AI Chats. Version .

ChatGPT and Claude don't make it easy to see when a conversation happened. ChatStamp adds the missing dates and times, so you can find a chat by when you had it and keep a copy when you need one.

What it does

Dates in the sidebarEvery chat shows when it started, including chats from before you installed ChatStamp.
A time on every messageSee the date and time above each message inside a chat.
Search across both sitesFind a chat by title, site or date from the toolbar popup, grouped by day or month.
Print any chat as PDFSave a clean copy of a chat, with its formatting, times and images.
Private by designNo server, no account and no analytics. Dates are stored only in this browser, and message text is never stored.
