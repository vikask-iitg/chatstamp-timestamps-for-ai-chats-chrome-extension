Your chats now have dates

ChatStamp adds a small date next to each chat in the ChatGPT and Claude sidebars, so you can find a conversation by when it happened.

What to expect on each site

  • ChatGPT and Claude: dates appear on all your chats, including ones from before you installed the extension. Reload any open tabs to see them.
  • A date starting with ~ is an estimate: the first time the extension saw that chat, not the moment it was created. Hover any date for the full date and time.

Your data stays here

Dates are stored only in this browser. The extension has no server, no account and no analytics, and it never reads or stores the text of your messages. It keeps each chat's site, ID, title and dates, nothing else.

You can change the date format or delete everything at any time in Settings.
